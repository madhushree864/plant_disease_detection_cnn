existing files `paper_dataset_verification.md` and

- full citation, authors, venue, year, and DOI;
- stated problem and research objectives;
- dataset identity, size, class definitions, and provenance;
- preprocessing, augmentation, architecture, and training protocol;
- evaluation metrics and baseline comparisons;
- reported results with page/table/figure references;
- strengths, reproducibility gaps, threats to validity, and relevance to this

No paper claims or numerical values are fabricated here.
